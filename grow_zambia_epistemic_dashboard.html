03 · S.N.A.K. and OMEGA checks

Epistemic control checklist

SEPARATE

Distinguish the target, measurement, source statement, interpretation and proposed cause.

NAME

Identify the institution, dataset, publication date, unit, geography and reporting period.

ASSIGN

Record who is responsible for delivery, data production, verification and correction.

KAVOD

Ask whether measurable outcomes support human flourishing: affordability, livelihoods, access and resilience.

Ω · Source synchronisation

Compare independent, traceable sources. Mark findings synchronised, desynchronised or unresolved; do not treat repeated copies of one claim as independent confirmation.

ΔD · Who benefits, who pays?

Where relevant, record distributional effects across provinces, rural/urban households, farmers, workers and consumers.

Methodology and limits: This dashboard does not certify official results. A value is not treated as verified merely because it appears on a government or media website. Check source provenance, definitions, units, time period, geography, methods and independent corroboration. Missing data are shown as missing. Progress is not calculated from non-comparable series. No official endorsement is implied.
